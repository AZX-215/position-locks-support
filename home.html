Position Locks for Katapult

Lock or unlock node positions in Katapult Maps, one node at a time or across all eligible nodes in an editable job.

How it works ↓

An independent extension for desktop Chrome.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open an editable job

    In desktop Chrome, open a Katapult Maps job you have permission to edit. Select a node on the map to work on it individually.

  2. 2

    Choose your scope

    Click Position Locks at the bottom of the map. Choose Open node or All eligible nodes in this job.

  3. 3

    Lock or unlock positions

    Click Lock Positions or Unlock Positions. Review the summary. During a batch, Stop ends the batch and keeps completed changes.

Supported node types Poles, references, building attachments, and break points.
